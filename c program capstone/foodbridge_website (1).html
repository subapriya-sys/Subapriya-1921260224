The purpose

Turning surplus into
something meaningful.

Food may be left unused while communities need support. FoodBridge organizes donation information so surplus food can be recorded, reviewed and allocated more clearly.

System modules

Simple modules.
Clear coordination.

The project is organized into two core modules that support donation entry, inventory management and recipient allocation.

🥬

Food Donation Entry & Inventory

Captures donor name, contact, food category, quantity, preparation or collection date and expiry information. Records can be displayed, saved and retrieved.

🤝

Recipient Matching & Allocation

Stores recipient or organization requirements, shows available food and supports allocation to a selected recipient while avoiding repeated allocation.

📋

Donation Status Tracking

Organizes each donation with a status such as Available, Allocated or Delivered, making the donation record easier to follow.

System flow

From entry to delivery.

A menu-driven flow guides the user through entering donations, reviewing records and updating their status.

STEP 01

Open main menu

Choose Add Donation, View Donations, Find Recipient, Update Status or Exit.

STEP 02

Enter food details

Record donor, food item, quantity and expiry information.

STEP 03

Review & allocate

Review available donations and assign suitable food to recipients.

STEP 04

Save & confirm

Save changes to files and show a confirmation or summary.
